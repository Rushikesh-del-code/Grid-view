Shinchan

Shin-chan is a famous five-year-old Japanese cartoon character named Shinnosuke Nohara, created by Yoshito Usui.

Masao

Masao Sato (often referred to as Masao-kun) is a prominent 5-year-old fictional character from the highly popular Japanese manga and anime series Crayon Shin-chan

Himawari

Himawari Nohara is Shinnosuke's (Shin-chan's) baby sister in the popular anime and manga series ⁠Crayon Shin-chan. Her name means "sunflower" in Japanese. She is a vibrant, clever, and mischievous infant who loves shiny jewels, expensive brand-name items, and handsome guys

Kazama

Toru Kazama (often called Kazama-kun) is Shin-chan's wealthy, highly polite, and intensely studious 5-year-old classmate at Futaba Kindergarten

Writer Name: Yoshito Usui

Yoshito Usui was the Japanese manga artist and writer who created the famous comedy series ⁠Crayon Shin-chan.
